# gutcheck

Terms for the structured judgments Jev makes about text.

## Language

**Noul**:
A yes/no question or statement answered with an estimated probability of true, from 0 to 1.

**Choice**:
A question answered by selecting one option from a defined set.

**Score**:
A question answered by the probability-weighted average of the numeric positions in an ordered rubric. The score may fall between rubric levels.
